About the Newsletter
The WebAIM newsletter is for people that are interested in receiving monthly news and updates on web accessibility issues. The newsletter will include featured articles, monthly tips, resources, and questions from WebAIM's discussion forum. The featured articles will be from WebAIM staff as well as other key figures in the web accessibility circles.
