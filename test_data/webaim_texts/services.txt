The WebAIM Strategy
WebAIM provides a wide variety of services, but our goal is always the same—to expand the potential of the web for people with disabilities by empowering individuals and organizations to create accessible content.
WebAIM services can include...
Training
WebAIM offers a variety of on-site and virtual
training options
, including:
Web accessibility virtual training
Online Document Accessibility course
Customized trainings for your organization
Accessibility in Technology Procurement and Use Workshop
to provide insight into tools and processes that will help any organization, large or small, better account for accessibility when making decisions about purchasing or using third-party technology.
We provide the knowledge, resources, and tools for your organization to meet its accessibility goals.
Consulting
Whether you are drafting wireframes, developing an accessibility policy, or implementing accessibility techniques and strategies, our expert staff can
consult
with you on-site or remotely.
Evaluation
Our team can inspect, audit, and
evaluate
a representative sample of pages from your site. Our website evaluation report will list the accessibility barriers we found, describe their impact on users, and recommend strategies for remediation. We can also evaluate designs, wireframes, and mockups to catch accessibility barriers before they find their way into your site or application.
No matter where you are at in your accessibility journey, WebAIM can be a guide to help you through the process.
Accessible Website Certification
WebAIM can provide a
conformance statement
that documents the accessibility of your site to established benchmarks of accessibility, such as
WCAG
.
