WebAIM: Example.com - Verification of
Conformance
Example.com
Verification of
Conformance
WebAIM, an independent authority in web accessibility, evaluated a representative sample of the
Example.com
web content and found
conformance with
Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)
Version 2.2 Level A and AA.
WebAIM cannot verify the conformance or accessibility of content beyond the scope of the sample, nor content changed after April 2, 2026. However, based on our interactions with the Example.com development team, WebAIM is confident in their ability and willingness to maintain
conformance.
